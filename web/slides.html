The Core Challenge

The Grassroots Feedback Chasm

Why multi-billion rupee infrastructure programs miss grassroots community needs.

Fragmented Citizen Distress

Rural and marginalized citizens communicate via localized voice notes, WhatsApp, or local dialects (Bhojpuri, Tamil, Bengali). Grievances remain buried in siloed portals with zero automated aggregation.

Problem: Broken water pipeline in Katihar takes 180+ days to reach the state infrastructure pipeline.

Disconnected Capital Allocation

District magistrates and planners allocate capital expenditure based on annual static budgetary quotas rather than live, verified demand hotspots and real-time deprivation indicators.

Impact: ₹1,000s of Crores deployed in low-impact areas while critical healthcare and water deficits persist.
The Gap: Governments lack a continuous, verified, multilingual translation layer connecting citizen pain points to verified public works projects.

Institutional Architecture

Git-Native OpenGAP Governance

Every agent behavior, invariant, tool, and audit trail lives in version-controlled Git files.

jansetu-ai/ ├── agent.yaml # Manifest (Gemini 2.5 Flash → 2.0 Flash → 1.5 Pro) ├── SOUL.md # Civic Identity & Non-partisan Values ├── RULES.md # Invariants (Zero PII, Zero Hallucination) ├── DUTIES.md # Maker-Checker Segregation of Duties ├── skills/ # Multilingual intake & demographic gap skills ├── tools/ # Deterministic PII Sanitizer & Capex Optimizer ├── knowledge/ # BRICS Census & Public Works Schedule of Rates ├── memory/runtime/ # Context & Cryptographic Audit Dailylog └── agents/verifier/ # Independent PolicyAuditor Checker Sub-Agent

Why Git-Native Governance Wins:

  • Immutable Audit Trails: Every project recommendation committed to Git history.
  • Segregation of Duties: The Maker (`CitizenAdvocate`) cannot self-authorize without Checker (`PolicyAuditor`) signature.
  • Zero SaaS Vendor Lock-in: Fully portable across runtimes via OpenGAP specification.
  • Zero-Trust PII Redaction: Aadhaar and phone numbers scrubbed before reasoning.
