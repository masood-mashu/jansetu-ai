# Compliance & Governance Artifacts

## 1. Digital Public Good (DPG) Standards
- Open Source Licensing: Apache 2.0.
- Data Privacy: Zero storage of unencrypted PII (Personally Identifiable Information).
- Non-discrimination & Accessibility: Compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA and Indian regional language support.

## 2. Responsible AI Guidelines (Google Cloud & MeitY)
- Fairness: Balanced representation across socio-economic and demographic cohorts.
- Transparency: Every recommendation includes an explainability trace and confidence metric.
- Safety: Guardrails block generation of harmful, toxic, or unverified claims.
- Human-in-the-loop: Critical administrative interventions require human officer authorization.
